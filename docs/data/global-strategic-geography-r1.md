# Global Strategic Geography R1 — source and precision notes

This Content derivative is a curated circa1900 strategic display snapshot. It does not provide global Local support, administrative membership, sovereignty, controller, navigability, transport capacity, market/factory locations or simulation routing. The sole Local shard remains Paris / Northern France.

## Transformations and limitations

- Settlement set curated from contemporary chief-city tables, map prominence, ports and railway centers. Display ranks are Presentation choices, not population facts. Validity1900-01-01 through1901-01-01 is a bounded display snapshot, not a foundation/closure claim. Representative coordinates generally have0.5–5km uncertainty, up to15km for metropolitan aliases. Paris shared coordinates/IDs are reused.
- Railways are **APPROXIMATE_STRATEGIC_CORRIDOR**. Named city connectivity is historical evidence; the dashed city-to-city polyline is **BOUNDED_ASSUMPTION**. Position errors can be tens or hundreds of kilometers. No surveyed track, station positions, companies, capacity or modern rail geometry is inferred. The New York–Chicago macro link is drawn via Philadelphia to avoid implying rail across Lake Erie. The1869 trunk stops at Oakland and passes Ogden; Salt Lake City is not placed on the original main line and the San Francisco ferry is not drawn as rail.
- Rivers include only Natural Earth's River class. Modern lake-centerline connectors are excluded. Named multi-record axes are grouped into a single stable feature (e.g. Yangtze includes Jinsha/Chang Jiang; Amazon includes Ucayali). Their source names are retained in the generator. This is a drainage-axis reference, not exact1900 banks or proof of navigability. Dams, diversions, channel movement and known generalized/suspect source alignment remain limitations.
- Lakes use selected natural Lake / Alkaline Lake records. Modern reservoirs, Aral remnants, Salton Sea and IJsselmeer are excluded. Lake levels and shoreline engineering are not reconstructed. Caspian is a physical water polygon, not a political territory.
- Offline Douglas–Peucker derivatives use coarse5000m and medium1500m tolerance. Closed lake rings are simplified as two arcs to retain area. LODs reference the same feature/fact; they are not new historical facts. Runtime never simplifies on camera movement.
- Raw JPEG scans, OCR, metadata and complete GIS sources remain in ignored `local-artifacts/global-strategic-r1/source/`; they are excluded from Windows Release. Only small processed Content shards are shipped.

## Scope and gaps

Nine geographic Content slices: Europe, Eurasia, East Asia, Japan, India, North America, South America, Africa and Oceania. These are loading bounds, not modern or historical administrative regions. Only current/nearby slices are active, bounded at two. Global medium coastline is **DEFER**; administration remains **ADMIN_BOUNDARY_DATA_GAP**. No road or terrain expansion is introduced.

The set is strategic and selective: it does not claim every capital, town or operating railway. Trans-Siberian Baikal/eastern links are omitted rather than fabricated as continuous. China is limited to the dated existing Pekin–Tientsin link. French Local railways retain their original9 identities/11 parts and are not duplicated into global rail facts. Paris water/settlement overlap is suppressed in Presentation; original Local source facts are preserved.

Natural Earth's named Dnepre River record only covers the upper reach (54.92–55.87°N). Lower engineered/reservoir-connected reaches are a **HYDROGRAPHY_DATA_GAP**; the dataset does not claim a complete Dnieper reconstruction. Volga and other regulated systems can likewise have breaks where modern lake-centerline connectors were excluded. Empty rural views at closer Region scales are expected for this deliberately selective backbone.
