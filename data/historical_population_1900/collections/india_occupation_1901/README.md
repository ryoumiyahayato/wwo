# India 1901 principal occupation orders

The source is Census of India, 1901, Vol. I-A, India, Part II: Tables, Government of India, 1903, Table XV. The note on printed p. 367 defines principal occupation, actual workers, dependents, and subsidiary agriculture. Part II p. 382 prints resident counts as `Total supported` and separately lists `Dependents`. Part III prints 24 principal occupation order totals in `Actual Workers`. The JSON locator gives each territory’s two printed pages, territory number, and column. The table uses residents of both sexes and all ages. [Census of India, 1901, official volume scan](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.55924).

Each row retains all 24 orders. `observed_counts` holds orders I–XXII. `census_table_controls` retains XXIII (the published combined Indefinite and Disreputable Occupations), XXIV (Independent, comprising Property and Alms and At the Public Charge), and the independent Part II dependent count. Add those three counts to obtain `observed_unknown`. Each Part III total equals Part II `total_supported` less `dependents`. The separate `Partially Agriculturist` columns are supplementary returns and outside the distribution.

Apply each row only on the matching territorial account. Central India Agency and Rajputana Agency replace the existing partial extracts. Agra and Oudh matches 47,691,782 residents in the historic British districts. Its opening aggregate directly retains a separately recorded, unlocated 294-person endpoint balance. Add that balance to the endpoint and unassigned count when allocating on the opening aggregate. Jurisdictions with no endpoint balance retain their original census denominator.
