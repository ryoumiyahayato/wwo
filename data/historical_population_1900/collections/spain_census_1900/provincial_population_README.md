# Spain provincial census controls

The 49 province rows are in the order printed in both censuses. Province numbers are source row identifiers. The common parent is `kingdom_of_spain`. Canarias is a single historical province.

The control is population present (`población de hecho`), including visitors and excluding temporarily absent residents. Source dates are 31 December 1897 and 31 December 1900. Read the final two columns for the matched opening scope, mainland Spain, the Balearic Islands and the Canary Islands. Both source tables also report African possessions separately, while counting Ceuta within Cádiz. Cádiz is corrected from 434,197 to 421,335 in 1897 and from 452,659 to 439,390 in 1900. The INE municipal census series supplies the two direct Ceuta exclusions. The two 49-province totals close at 18,108,610 and 18,594,405.
