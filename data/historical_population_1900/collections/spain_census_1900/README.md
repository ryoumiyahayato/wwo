# Spain census 1900

Date is 1900-12-31. The source counts population present, including visitors and excluding temporarily absent residents. The original national age table includes northern and western African possessions. Its 18,618,086 people exclude the separately published Guinea tables.

| Panel suffix | Scope | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nacional | Peninsula, Canary and Balearic Islands, and northern and western African possessions | Census Vol. III pp. 296–297 |
| ceuta | Ceuta | Census Vol. III pp. 64–65 |
| melilla | Melilla | Census Vol. III pp. 292–293 |
| otras_posesiones | Alhucemas, Chafarinas, Peñón de la Gomera and Río de Oro | Census Vol. III pp. 292–293 |
| peninsula_islas | Peninsula, Canary and Balearic Islands | National row minus Ceuta, Melilla and other possessions, cell by cell |

Population controls, each sex's age rows, and each sex's marital counts reconcile. The clean-scope control is 18,594,405 (9,071,965 male, 9,522,440 female). Unknown ages and marital status are separate. The open age class starts at 101. Age and marital status are independent opening marginals.

The opening target is `kingdom_of_spain` on 1900-01-01. The 18,511,807 opening population uses the existing Federico–Tena national estimate for the same geography. Applying December census shares to January is a model. Census error and change in shares between the two dates are unquantified.
