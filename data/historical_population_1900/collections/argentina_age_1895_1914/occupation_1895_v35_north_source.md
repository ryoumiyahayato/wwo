# Argentine occupational census, Catamarca and Jujuy, 1895

The Argentine census of 10 May 1895, Tomo II, Cuadros XXVI a–d, publishes the four nationality and sex columns and an all-person total for the population aged 14 and older. Catamarca is on [printed pp. 513–516, PDF pp. 709–712](https://biblioteca.indec.gob.ar/bases/minde/1c1895t2.pdf#page=709). Jujuy is on [printed pp. 624–627, PDF pp. 820–823](https://biblioteca.indec.gob.ar/bases/minde/1c1895t2.pdf#page=820). The independently printed [Cuadro XI, PDF p. 176](https://biblioteca.indec.gob.ar/bases/minde/1c1895t2.pdf#page=176) reports the same age-scope totals of 53,369 and 32,337.

Catamarca closes to all four Cuadro XI columns (22,382 / 30,019 / 701 / 267 in Argentine men, Argentine women, foreign men, foreign women order). For Jujuy, the printed XXVI columns (14,204 / 13,814 / 3,089 / 1,230) close internally and differ from XI (14,203 / 13,815 / 3,089 / 1,230) by +1 Argentine man and −1 Argentine woman. The occupational marginal uses only the printed total persons. No occupation-by-sex or nationality distribution is allocated.

The source reports 1,658 Hacendados, 207 teachers and 142 students in Catamarca, and 2,294 Hacendados, 125 teachers and 86 students in Jujuy. Existing occupation admission rules apply. Census all-age provincial totals are 90,161 and 49,713 respectively.
