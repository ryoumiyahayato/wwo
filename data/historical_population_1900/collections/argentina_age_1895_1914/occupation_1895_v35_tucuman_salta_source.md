# Argentine occupational census, Tucumán and Salta, 1895

The Argentine census of 10 May 1895, Tomo II, Cuadros XXVI a–d, publishes tabulated professions for the Argentine and foreign population aged at least 14. Tucumán is on [printed pp. 552–555, PDF pp. 748–751](https://biblioteca.indec.gob.ar/bases/minde/1c1895t2.pdf#page=748). Salta is on [printed pp. 592–595, PDF pp. 788–791](https://biblioteca.indec.gob.ar/bases/minde/1c1895t2.pdf#page=788).

The 18 printed census classes in each province close to the four cells of [Cuadro XI, PDF p. 176](https://biblioteca.indec.gob.ar/bases/minde/1c1895t2.pdf#page=176). In Argentine men, Argentine women, foreign men, foreign women order these are 62,575 / 60,085 / 6,665 / 2,467 for Tucumán and 32,510 / 35,519 / 2,799 / 1,381 for Salta. The age-scope totals are 131,792 and 72,209. The 1895 all-age census counts are 215,742 and 118,015.

Existing occupation admission rules apply. The printed Hacendados, teachers and students are 1,027 / 360 / 146 in Tucumán and 2,561 / 180 / 72 in Salta.
