# Argentina 1895 census religion

`religion_1895.csv` transcribes the four totals for each of the 24 individual geographic rows in *Segundo Censo de la República Argentina, Mayo 10 de 1895*, Tomo II, *Población* (1898), Cuadros XIX a/b, printed pp. CLXXVIII–CLXXIX. In the INDEC scan these are the 184th and 185th pages. The source labels are Católicos, Protestantes, todas las confesiones, Israelitas, and Otras religiones. Source dashes in these count columns are transcribed as zero. The `observed_total` is the sum of the four printed category totals and is checked against the individually enumerated geography. [INDEC scan](https://biblioteca.indec.gob.ar/bases/minde/1c1895t2.pdf#page=184)

The 10 May 1895 census comprises 3,954,911 individually enumerated residents in these 24 rows. The printed national controls are 3,921,136 Católicos, 26,750 Protestantes, 6,085 Israelitas and 940 Otras religiones. The row names follow the source. Four source units map together to `ar_central_chaco_comparable` (Córdoba, Santa Fé, Santiago del Estero and Chaco), with a combined denominator of 920,335. The opening Puna account and the unlocated population reserve have no corresponding individually tabulated 1895 religion observations.

The field instruction asked enumerators to inquire only where they had reason to think a person was not Catholic. The Catholic count consequently includes presumed Catholics. The report combines other faiths and declarations of no religion in Otras religiones. Retain that reported count in the raw table and assign it to unknown for formal religion allocation. This treatment preserves the printed accounting and does not correct the effect of the questionnaire. The 1914 national census did not ask the question. The 1895 to 1900 change and the error of a same-scope opening projection are unmeasured.
