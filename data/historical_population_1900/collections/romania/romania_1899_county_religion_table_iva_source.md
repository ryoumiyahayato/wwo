# Romanian Old Kingdom county religion, December 1899

The extract retains the 32 county rows in Colescu's Table IV.A for the stable population of urban and rural communes. Its opening controls agree with Table II.A and the independent county all-age controls. See the regional transcription for category names, month precision, and geography. [Table IV.A](https://archive.org/details/recensamantulgen00serv/page/n162/mode/2up)

Ilfov and Olt have opposite one-person discrepancies in the female subtotals. Table IV.A prints 28 and 12 respectively for `Alta și necunoscută`. Table IV.B–D's mutually exclusive territorial compartments give Ilfov 20 in București, zero in Oltenița and 7 in rural communes, and Olt 2 in Slatina and 11 in rural communes. The seven named religion counts agree with the compartment totals for both counties. The reconciled unknown counts are therefore 27 in Ilfov and 13 in Olt. The CSV also retains the value as printed. Both the raw and reconciled unknown counts sum to the same Muntenia control.
