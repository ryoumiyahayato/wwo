# Romanian Old Kingdom religion, December 1899

Leonida Colescu and Serviciul Statisticei Generale, *Recensământul general al populațiunei României din decembrie 1899* (1905), Table IV.A, printed pp. 72–73, one-based PDF pp. 162–163. The table covers the stable population of urban and rural communes. Its four regional rows and their sex totals match the existing opening accounts and the age table. `Dobrogea` refers to the historic Romanian territory of Northern Dobruja. [Table IV.A](https://archive.org/details/recensamantulgen00serv/page/n162/mode/2up)

In CSV order, the source reports `Ortodoxă`, `Mozaică`, `Catolică`, `Protestantă`, `Mahometană`, `Armeană`, `Lipovană`, and `Alta și necunoscută`. The labels name religions in this table. The final column combines other religious affiliations and unknown responses. Assign it to the unknown count. The male and female control columns verify source scope. The extract provides a religion marginal for each region.

The national row prints 5,451,787 Orthodox, 266,652 Jewish, 149,667 Catholic, 22,749 Protestant, 44,732 Muslim, 5,787 Armenian, 15,094 Lipovan, and 222 other or unknown, totaling 5,956,690. Each national category equals the sum of the four regional rows. The four accounts are the units for allocation.

The December 1899 census has month precision. Enumeration took place across December and reached early January Gregorian in Ialomița. The January 1 opening uses the existing near-date baseline with timing uncertainty. The source is already registered as `ro_colescu_1899_census_report`.
