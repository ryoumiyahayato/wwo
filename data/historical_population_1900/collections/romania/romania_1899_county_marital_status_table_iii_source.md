# Romanian Old Kingdom county marital status, December 1899

Leonida Colescu's census report gives marital status for stable residents of urban and rural communes in all 32 counties. Table III.1.A groups children with never-married residents (`Copii și necăsătoriții`). Tables III.2–4.A cover `Căsătoriții` (married), `Văduvii` (widowed) and `Divorțații` (divorced). The CSV captures the printed male, female and both-sex total columns on pp. 35, 49, 57 and 65. Each sex total includes residents of unknown age. [III.1.A](https://archive.org/details/recensamantulgen00serv/page/n125/mode/1up), [III.2.A](https://archive.org/details/recensamantulgen00serv/page/n139/mode/1up), [III.3.A](https://archive.org/details/recensamantulgen00serv/page/n147/mode/1up), [III.4.A](https://archive.org/details/recensamantulgen00serv/page/n155/mode/1up)

The four categories close to Table II.A by sex in every county, and to all regional and national Table III controls. The national counts are 3,280,185 children and never married, 2,294,277 married, 365,082 widowed and 17,146 divorced. The sum is 5,956,690. No resident is outside the classified counts. Introductory Tables 9 and 10 (pp. XXXI–XXXII) give the same national totals. Use the 32 county rows as the exclusive partition. [Table II.A](https://archive.org/details/recensamantulgen00serv/page/n103/mode/1up)

For Romanați, Table III.3.A prints 3,978 men and 6,464 women in the widowed category, with a printed both-sex total of 10,440. The CSV preserves 10,440 in `widowed_printed_total` and uses 10,442 from the sex columns in `widowed`. This sum closes to the county and the regional and national subtotals.
