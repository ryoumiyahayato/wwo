# Surigao opening population

The 1898 official guide lists 85,125 people for the Spanish Third District.
The Philippine Commission reprints the figure in Volume III, p. 111. The
War Department gazetteer reproduces it and notes that Indigenous groups
outside the regular returns are excluded. The guide's publication year
provides the interpolation anchor and the underlying count has no known
reference day. [Philippine Commission, Volume III, p. 111](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4d/Report_of_the_Philippine_commission_to_the_President._-_January_31%2C_1900%28-December_20%2C_1900%29_%28IA_aex9637.0001.003.umich.edu%29.pdf) · [War Department gazetteer, Surigao footnote](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f0/A_pronouncing_gazetteer_and_geographical_dictionary_of_the_Philippine_islands_.._%28IA_pronouncinggazet00unit%29.pdf)

Act 127 adopted the Spanish Third District as Surigao Province. The
1903-03-02 census main schedule counts 99,298 across 34 municipalities,
including Butuan. A separate schedule adds 15,814 at the 1903 date.
Act 787 altered the province after the census, and Act 1693 detached
Agusan in 1907. The nationwide opening balance retains all people
outside the modeled main-schedule population. The two endpoints preserve
their own enumerated scopes. [Act 127](https://lawphil.net/statutes/acts/act1901/act_127_1901.html) · [1903 census Volume II, Tables 1 and 3](https://www2.census.gov/library/publications/decennial/island-areas/1903/1903-census-phillipine-islands-vol2.pdf) · [Act 787](https://lawphil.net/statutes/acts/act1903/act_787_1903.html) · [Act 1693](https://lawphil.net/statutes/acts/act1907/act_1693_1907.html)

Linear interpolation from the midpoint of the 1898 publication year
gives 89,691 people at opening. Moving the assumed early date within
that calendar year gives 88,535 to 90,614 people and measures date
sensitivity only. The inherited Plehn control of 6,961,339 leaves
6,871,648 people geographically unlocated. [U.S. Census Bulletin 149, p. 16](https://www2.census.gov/library/publications/decennial/1900/bulletins/operations/149-methods-results-area-population.pdf)

Table 18, pp. 382–383, gives 24 age bands by sex for the main schedule.
The bands add to 50,640 males and 48,658 females. Both counts match Table 5,
p. 210, and sum to 99,298. Opening uses the 1903 shares on the modeled
1900 population. Table 53, p. 866, gives five groups of gainful occupations totaling
37,974. The 61,324 recorded as not gainful or unknown remain unassigned.
The six source columns combined match the all-age main schedule by sex.
Opening applies the shares as an independent occupation marginal.
