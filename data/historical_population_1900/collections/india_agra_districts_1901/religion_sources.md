# Agra district religion, 1901

`agra_religion_1901.csv` transcribes the 36 districts, seven division controls and the N.-W. Provinces control for persons on 1 March 1901. Use `row_role=district` for the 36 leaf accounts. The column headings are Table VI column 3 population, column 6 Hindu, column 9 Sikh, column 12 Jain, column 15 Buddhist, column 18 Parsi, column 21 Muslim, column 24 Christian, column 27 Jewish, column 30 Arya and column 33 Brahmo. Printed pp. 38, 39 and 40 are scanned in PDF order 42, 44 and 43. Printed p. 37 lists two New Hindu Vedantists in Almora, one male and one female. That count is carried in a separate column and included in the Almora, Kumaun and provincial totals. [Table VI notes](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/31349/download/34530/140099_1901.pdf#page=41), [Table VI district rows and Hindu column](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/31349/download/34530/140099_1901.pdf#page=42), [Table VI columns 9–23](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/31349/download/34530/140099_1901.pdf#page=44), [Table VI columns 24–35](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/31349/download/34530/140099_1901.pdf#page=43).

The 36 district rows match the Table I population and sum by religion to the published divisions and province. Dots in the count columns are transcribed as zero. Keep the main Allahabad row 23, with 1,489,358 enumerated persons, and the main Fyzabad row in Oudh. The fair-adjusted alternate rows apply a different allocation. The Agra control here is 34,858,705. The separate 294-person Table II-A balance has no verified religion fields. [Table I controls](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/31349/download/34530/140099_1901.pdf#page=7), [Table VI fair note](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/31349/download/34530/140099_1901.pdf#page=41).
