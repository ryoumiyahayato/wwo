# Egypt 1897 Gharbiya occupation status and Jirja literacy

The source is the Egyptian government’s 1 June 1897 census, published at the National Printing Office in 1898. The archived data come from Hirofumi Tanada’s transcription in Appendix 2-1 (MARKAZ). The 1897 original printing page numbers for these subtotals could not be verified online.

Gharbiya reports 428,325 males and 6,213 females in the `with occupation` column. The remaining 223,406 males and 639,712 females appear in `without`. These are reported occupation statuses. The source gives no breakdown of persons without work, respondents whose work was unrecorded, or employees versus employers. The census summary includes servants among the people reporting occupations.

Jirja reports 15,987 males and 88 females in `READ&WRITE` and 333,638 males and 338,298 females in `ILLITERATE`. The denominator is the total population of all ages. The source gives no separate schooling or age threshold within these counts.

Both rows close independently to the province population by sex. The official 1907 comparative Table 5 (Arabic edition, printed pages 31 and 32) confirms the corresponding 1897 persons, males and females controls. These are control pages for the 1897 population, and the 1897 classification values come from the transcription.
