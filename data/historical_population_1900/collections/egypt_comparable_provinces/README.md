# Egypt 1907 Census Table 5

The Ministry of Finance Census Department published the 1907 census in Cairo in 1909. The Arabic edition uses printed pages 29–32 for Table 5. Dates are 1 June 1897 and 29 April 1907.

- Page 29 compares the original 1897 province totals with the 1897 figures adjusted by the 1907 census. It lists all 14 mudiriyas and a grouped urban governorate total. The six province controls in the CSV match both 1897 columns. Minya changes from 548,632 to 550,971 and is outside this extract. [Page 29](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco003741/55/full/1600,/0/default.jpg)
- Page 30 places Cairo and Alexandria in the separate urban governorate panel. The province controls in the CSV come from the lower and upper Egyptian province panels. [Page 30](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco003741/56/full/1600,/0/default.jpg)
- Page 31 contains Gharbiya, Menufiya and Asyut. Page 32 contains Faiyum, Jirja and Qena. Each row has the original printed total and male and female counts. [Page 31](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco003741/57/full/1600,/0/default.jpg) [Page 32](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco003741/58/full/1600,/0/default.jpg)

Page 29 explains the differences in geography and includes estimated Bedouin people in the 1907 comparative totals where indicated. The asterisk identifies nomadic tribes included in an individual row. It marks Tala in Menufiya, Dirut in Asyut, Balyana in Jirja, and Dishna and Qus in Qena. The provincial subtotals already include these residents. The 1907 figures should be used on their own with the corresponding Table 5 1897 rows, since the routine markaz transcription has different coverage.

Asyut includes Kharga and Dakhla oases and Mallawi according to rows 55, 56 and 58. Jirja includes the five comparative districts in rows 80–84. The 1907 census retrospectively groups 1897 settlements into the same five districts. Qena includes Esna, Luxor and Quseir within its seven rows. None of these provincial accounting scopes includes either of the existing Cairo and Alexandria city counts.

Faiyum has identical province and district totals in the original and adjusted 1897 columns available through the archive and transcription. The comparative table establishes statistical comparability. It supplies no geometric crosswalk to 1900 boundaries.
