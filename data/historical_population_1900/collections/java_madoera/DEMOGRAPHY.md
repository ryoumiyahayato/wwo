# Java and Madura population returns, 1895–1905

## 1900 opening scope

The existing `java_madoera_colonial_population` opening account covers 28,092,332 civilians on 1 January 1900. It is calculated from the 31 December 1896 and 1900 returns using the elapsed fraction 274/365. Both returns include the Djokjakarta and Soerakarta residencies and exclude army and naval personnel. Coverage of private estates is incomplete. [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1900, II.I.A, printed p. 2](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-111_No-01.pdf#page=23).

The full-territory 1900 revised Indigenous row in the 1904 yearbook III.III has 13,913,849 males and 14,472,272 females. It closes against the 28,386,121 revised Indigenous total in III.I.A. The contemporary provisional Indigenous return used to derive the opening control is 28,384,731, a difference of 1,390. III.III has a footnote excluding Cheribon private estates and cites 148,730 estate residents. The 1895 edition has the same footnote and the regional table omits this population exactly. The revised 1900 Indigenous adult/child and geographic summaries use the same aggregate. Keep the private-estate coverage qualification. The III.III regions use the boundaries after five mergers effective 1 January 1901. [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1904, III.I.A and III.III, printed pp. 2 and 4](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-122_No-01.pdf#page=26).

The same edition's III.V total line `Totaal incl. aand. a.` gives 32,942 males and 29,535 females at the end of 1900 under `a = Java—Madura`. The term `Europeesche en met deze gelijkgestelde bevolking` identifies a legal classification. The sexes close against the 62,477 same-territory civilians in III.I.A. [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1904, III.V, printed p. 5](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-122_No-01.pdf#page=27).

Interpolate each of the five provisional categories using the existing opening endpoints and largest-remainder integer apportionment. Indigenous 27,738,313. European and legally assimilated 59,799. Chinese 273,275. Arab 17,800. Other Foreign Oriental 3,145. Apply the respective 31 December 1900 sex shares to the first two opening populations. Rounded male counts are 13,596,317 and 31,530. Female counts are 14,141,996 and 28,269. The v31 sex-only opening estimate is 13,627,847 males, 14,170,265 females and 294,220 sex unknown. v31 leaves age bounds unknown. The 1900 returns are observations. Mapping their sex proportions to 1 January 1900 is an estimate.

The government decree of 14 April 1900 no. 126 instructed enumeration of the named civilian populations at headquarters, villages, hamlets and settlements. Earlier instructions had paired enumeration with assessment of labour obligations and livestock. The sex columns refer to population. [Contemporary interpretation and decree, De Economist 1902, printed pp. 337–338](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/BF02286697.pdf#page=1).

## Regional age labels and timing

The 1895 table includes 22 residency rows and island totals. The age class is absent for Djokjakarta. Its sexes are present. The reported totals 12,386,701 males and 12,835,114 females sum across the regions. Adding the omitted 148,730 Cheribon estate residents gives the published full Indigenous count 25,370,545. The 1900 table lists 17 residencies after absorbing Krawang into Batavia, Tegal into Pekalongan, Japara into Semarang, Bagelen into Kedoe and Probolinggo into Pasoeroean. [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1900, II.III, printed p. 4](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-111_No-01.pdf#page=25), [1904 edition, III.I.A and III.III](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-122_No-01.pdf#page=26).

The linked 1895 and 1900 scans retain their original age-class columns. The whole-island 1900 age-by-sex cells close to both sex totals. Some regional age cells and the Madioen female total have scan transcription inconsistencies. Use the separately reconciled regional controls for sex. The original regional adult and child columns remain research data. The published labels `Volwassenen` and `Kinderen` carry no numeric threshold in either table. Administrative practice could classify young men according to work obligation. This is insufficient to substitute 16 for the age boundary. Use only the sex margins. [CBS, 1904 edition, III.III](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-122_No-01.pdf#page=26).

## 1905 scope and age lead

The 1905 return is nominally year-end. Enumeration happened at different dates. Officials mostly brought returns to year-end with registers of changes. Soerakarta did not keep the corresponding registers. Some Batavia returns counted close to year-end were left as taken. The Colonial Report omits both prince states only from its directly ruled residency growth subtotal. The yearly CBS series includes both. Its III.I.A and III.II close to 30,098,008. The Colonial Report opening comparison has an Indigenous count of 29,969,005, 253,097 above the CBS Indigenous table. Keep the two denominators separate. The Colonial Report also records 9,502 privately estimated Demak residents omitted from the Semarang count. The 1908 report names cholera, fevers, food shortage, emigration and changing procedures behind the 1900–1905 discrepancies. These changes weaken a constant age composition assumption. [Koloniaal Verslag 1907, opening cols. 1–2](https://repository.overheid.nl/frbr/sgd/19071908/0000353862/1/pdf/SGD_19071908_0001730.pdf), [Koloniaal Verslag 1908, opening section](https://repository.overheid.nl/frbr/sgd/19081909/0000351540/1/pdf/SGD_19081909_0001662.pdf), [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1906, III.I–II](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-120_No-01.pdf).

CBS 1906 edition III.IX, printed pp. 8–9, publishes age by sex and five legal population classes by district. It uses `Beneden 16 jaar`, `16–50 jaar` and `50 jaar en ouder`. The last two bands share the printed boundary at 50. Their combined count gives ages 16 and over. The source has reported exclusions for the Outer Islands, including Tapanoeli and numerous missing cells. It gives no complete age total for the Outer Islands. The 1900 yearbook documents no Indigenous enumeration in Timor and carries forward some 1895 rows. Atjeh was still under active contestation, and source ranges cannot be matched to the opening arithmetic balance of 16,271,347. [CBS, 1906 edition III.I.B and III.IX](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-120_No-01.pdf), [CBS, 1900 edition II.I.B](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-111_No-01.pdf#page=24), [Koloniaal Verslag 1907, chapter C](https://repository.overheid.nl/frbr/sgd/19071908/0000353862/1/pdf/SGD_19071908_0001730.pdf).


## Matched Java and Madoera age estimates

The v33 opening uses the 17 formal geography accounts described in [GEOGRAPHY.md](GEOGRAPHY.md). Male counts are apportioned from local Indigenous population times the revised local male fraction, normalized to the fixed island-wide Indigenous male total. Female counts are the remaining local Indigenous population. Ten local age distributions cover 17,078,108 people. The island-wide sex totals stay unchanged. The older aggregate-only model below describes frozen v32.

The reconciled 1900 locality controls report 17 mutually exclusive residencies. The 1900 reference uses geographic definitions effective 1 January 1901. Batavia includes Krawang, Pekalongan includes Tegal, Semarang includes Japara, Kedoe includes Bagelen and Pasoeroean includes Probolinggo. The 1905 tables use these same aggregate names. The included ages use the first, second, third and fifth of those merged rows. Kedoe remains in the age-unknown balance. No absorbed unit is counted again. [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1904, III.I.A and III.III, printed pp. 2 and 4](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-122_No-01.pdf), [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1906, III.I.A and III.IX, printed pp. 2 and 8–9](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-120_No-01.pdf).

The 17 sex controls close to 13,913,849 males and 14,472,272 females. The 1900 Madioen web transcription reads 619,919 females. Its two printed female cells, the regional total 1,227,761 less 608,542 males, and the island female total less the other 16 regions all give 619,219. The controlled 17-locality sum is 28,386,121. The provisional 1900 edition used for the opening control reports 28,384,731 Indigenous civilians. Its end-1900 all-class total is 28,745,698. The revised Indigenous difference is 1,390. The revised edition prints an all-class total of 28,746,638. That is 940 above the provisional all-class total. The later edition's five class figures themselves sum to 28,747,028. Keep the opening account sourced to the provisional endpoints. [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1900, II.I.A, printed p. 2](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-111_No-01.pdf), [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1904, III.I.A and III.III, printed pp. 2 and 4](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-122_No-01.pdf).

Ten 1905 Indigenous residency rows have six observed age-by-sex cells that close to their population controls. They comprise 18,532,424 people in 1905 and 17,491,664 in the 1900 sex controls. The 1905 Semarang population cell reads 2,517,492 in the web transcription. The categories and aggregate residents for Semarang, the 17-residency Indigenous sum, the six age cells and the Colonial Report opening table all give 2,571,492. The six cells for Kedoe and Kediri have differences of 400 and 100,000 against the corresponding totals. Cheribon age cells are incomplete. Djokjakarta, Madioen and Madoera give age margins for the sexes combined. Banjoemas also has a 2,000-person inconsistency. All seven retain age unknown. [CBS, Jaarcijfers Koloniën 1906, III.I.A and III.IX, printed pp. 2 and 8–9](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-120_No-01.pdf), [Koloniaal Verslag 1907, cols. 1–2](https://repository.overheid.nl/frbr/sgd/19071908/0000353862/1/pdf/SGD_19071908_0001730.pdf).

The model keeps the existing opening Indigenous controls of 13,596,317 males and 14,141,996 females. Within each sex, allocate these counts over the 17 regions by their shares of the corresponding 31 December 1900 sex total. Use largest remainders, with alphabetical region order for ties. For each matched row and sex, multiply its opening allocation by its own 31 December 1905 under-16 share and round halves up. Assign the rest to ages 16 and over. The time shift assumes that each matched region's age composition within sex can approximate its 1 January 1900 composition. Nominal dates differ by six calendar years. Incomplete estates, disease, food shortages, migration and changing enumeration methods can affect the approximation. This also applies to the Semarang estimate. Soerakarta's 1905 update lacked a continuous register.

The matched model covers 17,092,483 people. The Java opening age groups combine the region estimates in the same account. The four estimated counts are 3,914,160 males under 16, 3,814,065 females under 16, 4,454,836 males 16 and over, and 4,909,422 females 16 and over. Another 5,258,851 males and 5,446,778 females have unknown age, including the European and legally assimilated population. Both attributes are unknown for 294,220. These seven counts sum to 28,092,332. The adult and child columns from 1900 were not used as numeric ages.

The **regional_controls** and **age_sex_matched** CSV files preserve the geographic controls and cited 1905 cells. The opening generator calculates the age groups from those tables. Age estimates are feasible only for the matched Indigenous subset. No opening age conclusion follows for the arithmetic Outer Islands balance.
