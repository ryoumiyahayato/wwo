# Java and Madoera residency populations, 1896 and 1900

`residency_census.csv` preserves all 22 original residency rows for 31 December 1896 and all 17 original residency rows for 31 December 1900. `unit_id` assigns each observation to one mutually exclusive opening statistical area. `residency_id` identifies the source area. The formal opening generator produces 17 integer estimates for 1 January 1900. It keeps the source spelling and the names of all residencies in force on the opening date.

The 1896 observations use CBS, *Jaarcijfers voor het Koninkrijk der Nederlanden: Koloniën, 1898*, Oost-Indië, II.I.A, printed p. 2, Java en Madoera. The 1899 edition already in the game independently prints the same aggregate and refers to the 1898 *Regeerings-Almanak voor Nederlandsch-Indië*. The new local source ID is `dutch_jaarcijfers_colonien_1898`. [CBS 1898, printed p. 2](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-113_No-01.pdf), [CBS 1899, printed p. 2](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-112_No-01.pdf#page=23).

The 1900 observations use the provisional edition already in the game, `dutch_jaarcijfers_colonien_1900`. Bantam's Chinese cell is 2,422. Its row total, the island's Chinese total and the revised yearbook independently give 2,422. The web OCR reads 2,492. The 1904 revised Indigenous tabulation raises the Batavia and Besoeki counts by 1,000 and 390 respectively. Retain the provisional counts for the geography endpoints and the revised regional sex proportions for Indigenous sex estimates. [CBS 1900, II.I.A, printed p. 2](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-111_No-01.pdf#page=23), [CBS 1904, III.I.A, printed p. 2](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-122_No-01.pdf#page=24).

The five combined areas use the reunified source boundaries from the 23 December 1900 measures in Staatsblad Nos. 334–335, effective 1 January 1901. The 1896 counts add the two printed residency areas in each pair. At opening both administrations still existed. Use the combined area as the statistical leaf and retain both names. The other 12 areas match directly across the two tables. The combined scopes do not imply a measured population split between the constituent areas in 1900. [CBS 1900, notes to II.I.A](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-111_No-01.pdf#page=23).

The five columns use the published legal registration classes. Both tables include Djokjakarta and Soerakarta, and the series excludes Army and Navy personnel. The returns document incomplete coverage of private estates, including the Indigenous Cheribon estate population. No estimate for missing residents has been added. The figures do not cover the Outer Possessions. The time interpolation and changing enumeration coverage limit the accuracy of the opening estimates. [CBS 1898, II.I.A](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-113_No-01.pdf), [CBS 1900, II.I.A and II.III](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-111_No-01.pdf#page=23), [CBS 1904, III.III](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-122_No-01.pdf#page=26).

The opening formula is `(91 * early + 274 * late) / 365` for each area and category. Floor all exact quotas for the category. Distribute the difference from the existing island category total by descending remainder, with ascending ASCII `unit_id` as the tie-break. The existing island account is a statistical aggregate over the leaves. The formal social ledger records the five registration categories as legal status. These counts carry no language, cultural, religious or occupational classification.

The 1904 revised end-1900 Indigenous sex controls and the 1905 broad-age ratios already archived in the game join on `source_residency_1900` to the archived `region`. They retain the combined-area boundaries. If both national sexes and exact local opening Indigenous totals are required, apportion male quotas proportional to each fixed local opening Indigenous total multiplied by its observed 1900 male fraction. Scale to the existing island Indigenous male total, use largest remainders, then take each local female count as the local total less males. This uses a sex-share proxy and does not substitute the revised 1900 geography totals. Ten matched areas give age estimates for 17,078,108 people. The categories `Volwassenen` and `Kinderen` in the 1900 table have no verified numeric age threshold. [CBS 1904, III.III](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-122_No-01.pdf#page=26), [CBS 1906, III.IX](https://seadelt.net/Asset/Source/Document_ID-120_No-01.pdf).

The checked historical map and place indexes contain no confirmed administrative ID with any of the 17 source footprints. The Batavia, Semarang, Soerabaja and Djokjakarta city observations are marked unmapped in the archival city collection. The game exposes the residency accounts by ID. The city observations remain separate research records.
