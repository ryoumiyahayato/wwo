# Beheira 1897–1907 population and age by sex

## Source observations

The Egyptian Ministry of Finance 1907 census gives the 1 June 1897 population on a comparison basis and the 29 April 1907 population. Table V p. 30 lists Beheira rows 8–18 separately. All 11 rows sum to 656,419 in 1897 (332,345 male and 324,074 female) and 830,015 in 1907 (414,549 male and 415,466 female). Table V includes the estimated nomadic population in starred rows. [Original Table V p. 30](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco003741/56/full/1600,/0/default.jpg) [Asterisk note p. 32](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco003741/58/full/1600,/0/default.jpg)

Table XI lists the same row numbers and gives counts by age, sex and census literacy status. The five standard age bands are 0–4, 5–9, 10–14, 15–19 and 20+. The printed person totals agree with the sum of the four cells in all 66 records, including controls. The arithmetic coverage for the 11 rows is 798,473, with 31,542 outside Table XI. Row 17 prints 0–14 as its first label and row 18 appears to print 14–19 as its fourth label. The CSV preserves those labels with blank machine-readable age bounds. ## Territorial comparability

The 1907 volume restates 1897 for changes in provincial and district boundaries and enumeration scope. Beheira's published 1897 total rises from 631,225 to 656,419. The difference of 25,194 reconciles to Ramleh (19,594), Siwa (5,200) and Wadi el Natrun (400). The adjusted 1897 Alexandria row excludes Ramleh. The Giza comparison drops from 401,634 to 401,234, consistent with the transfer of Wadi el Natrun printed on p. 29. Table V does not identify a separate Beheira Wadi el Natrun row. The core candidate uses the adjusted district returns as printed. Keep Ramleh as its separate existing opening account of 24,335. [Comparison note p. 29](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco003741/55/full/1600,/0/default.jpg) [District comparison p. 30](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco003741/56/full/1600,/0/default.jpg)

Marsa Matruh–Maryut–El Dabaa reports 330 in the 1897 comparison and 35,706 in 1907, including an estimated nomadic population. The 1907 local age table represents 7,805. The coverage of the earlier 330 gives no basis for a local opening model. Siwa reports 5,200 in the adjusted 1897 comparison and 3,884 in 1907. The 1907 age table closes to 3,884. The rounded 1897 inclusion and the age-label ambiguity need independent resolution before opening use. Both rows remain dated observations. ## Opening model for eight markaz

The eight Beheira district controls entering v37 are rows 8–10 and 12–16 in `table_5_sex_scope_controls.csv`. The 48 age-band and total rows are in `table_xi_opening_age_sex.csv`. Their Table V counts total 631,295 in 1897 and 752,662 in 1907. The opening falls 944 days after the earlier census in an interval of 3,618 days. Linearly interpolate each total and apportion each sex to its rounded local opening population. Allocate the opening sex balances to local age bands using 1907 shares of each Table V sex total. Allocate the Table V minus Table XI difference to unknown age in the same apportionment. Kafr al-Dawwar omits 500 males and 150 females in 1907. Kom Hamada omits 1,423 males and 1,568 females. Literacy applies to ages five and older within the same Table V denominator.

The eight units add 662,962 opening people, 333,385 male and 329,577 female. The local model assigns 659,800 to the five age bands and leaves 3,162 age unknown, 1,663 male and 1,499 female. Any partial Beheira statistical rollup must sum its admitted children without adding a population balance. The Egypt country control remains 10,000,000. District polygons have not been independently reconstructed. The age shares come from 2,674 days after opening. Local migration, age reporting and coverage changes have no quantified error interval.
