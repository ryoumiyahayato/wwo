# Bengal district literacy, 1901

The census date is 1901-03-01. This batch covers nine districts and 17,503,733 people. Use the `All Religions` row in Vol. VI-A, Imperial Table VIII, Education, Part B. The mutually exclusive `Literate` and `Illiterate` totals cover all ages. The census counted people able to read and write a letter. Imperial Tables I and II independently give the district population by sex. `Tippera district` belongs to British Territory. The census lists the princely `Hill Tippera` separately. [Census scan](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.55931). [Searchable transcription](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.55931/2015.55931.Census-Of-India-1901-Vol6-a-lower-Probinces-Of-Bengal-And-Their-Leudatoties-Pt2-imperial-Tables_djvu.txt).

The searchable transcription sometimes misreads digits. For Darjeeling, the two literate sex cells sum to 17,442 and the four female literate age cells sum to 1,662. The female complement is 114,450. For Mymensingh, the four literate age cells sum to 146,386. For Tippera and Noakhali, the literate and illiterate sex cells each recover the independent population. For Shahabad, the total literate and illiterate cells sum to 1,962,696. The male population less 856,271 illiterate males gives 80,273 literate males. The female complement within the literate category is 3,322. For Saran, the four age cells sum to 80,485 literate men and 2,695 literate women. For Muzaffarpur they sum to 102,815 and 4,750, for Monghyr 58,570 and 2,157, and for Malda 32,170 and 923. Each combined value agrees with the all-person literacy pair after identifying damaged OCR digits.
