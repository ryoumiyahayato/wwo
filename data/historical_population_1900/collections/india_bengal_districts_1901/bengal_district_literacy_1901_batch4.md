# Bengal district literacy, fourth batch

This batch covers 7 districts with 7,303,496 people on 1 March 1901. Imperial Table VIII, Education, Part B reports `Literate` and `Illiterate` for all ages in its `All Religions` rows. The census counted people able to read and write a letter. Tables I and II supply matching district population denominators by sex. [Gokhale Institute copy of the census tables](https://dspace.gipe.ac.in/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10973/18817/GIPE-017256.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3).

Compare the two available OCR transcriptions. For Nadia, the four all-person ages give 93,375, the complement of 1,574,116 illiterate. The female illiterate count of 832,714 gives 7,268 literate women. For Murshidabad, 73,476 literate and 1,259,708 illiterate agree with the independent denominator. The male and female literate counts also close to this total. The archive transcription omits most or all literacy cells for Chittagong, the Hill Tracts, Puri, Ranchi and Palamau. The Gokhale Institute copy preserves those cells. For Chittagong, the 76,106 male literates also follow from the male illiterate count of 565,286 in the archive transcription. The Hill Tracts total of 5,576 literate closes with 119,186 illiterate. For Ranchi, 607,839 female illiterates imply 2,906 literate women. In Palamau, 294,769 male illiterates imply 11,434 literate men. [Archive copy of the census tables](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.55931).
