# Bengal district literacy, second batch

This batch covers 16 districts with 24,903,545 people on 1 March 1901. The census counted people able to read and write a letter. Imperial Table VIII, Education, Part B gives mutually exclusive `Literate` and `Illiterate` columns for people of all ages in its `All Religions` rows. Tables I and II give the independent district totals by sex. `24-Parganas` is separate from Calcutta. [Provincial Census Vol. VI-A](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.55931).

Use readable printed cells and add the four literate age cells where the OCR has damaged digits. For 24-Parganas the female age cells add to 12,409. The male age 20 and over cell is 150,613 from population 584,555 less 433,942 illiterate. The all-person illiterate total of 1,845,739 gives 232,620 literate and 220,211 literate men. In Jalpaiguri, the female age cells add to 1,301 and the male and female illiterate counts agree with their population controls. In Champaran, the age cells add to 39,435 men and 1,061 women. In Darbhanga they add to 100,450 and 2,178. Bhagalpur’s 67,720 literate men and 1,540 literate women follow from the complement of the independently matched illiterate cells. For Angul and Khondmals, the four female age cells give 112 and the total is 3,840. Where needed, the remaining digit errors follow from the independent sex counts and the complementary categories.
