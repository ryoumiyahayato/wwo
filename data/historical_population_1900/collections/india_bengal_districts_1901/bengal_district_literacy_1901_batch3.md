# Bengal district literacy, third batch

This batch covers 7 districts with 10,333,615 people on 1 March 1901. Imperial Table VIII, Education, Part B gives mutually exclusive `Literate` and `Illiterate` columns for people of all ages in its `All Religions` rows. The census counted people able to read and write a letter. Tables I and II give the independent district totals by sex. [Provincial Census Vol. VI-A](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.55931).

For Hooghly, the four illiterate age rows give 423,969 men and 513,683 women. For Jessore, the total of 105,102 literate and the male illiterate count of 813,054 fix both literacy categories by sex using the Table I and II controls. For Khulna, the four literate age counts sum to 86,385. The illiterate total of 1,166,658 and its male count of 572,146 imply the separately tabulated 5,061 literate women. For Rajshahi, the four age rows give 2,588 literate women. The literate and illiterate totals of 62,240 and 1,400,167 imply 59,652 literate men and match the male illiterate count of 682,038. [Provincial Census Vol. VI-A OCR](https://archive.org/stream/in.ernet.dli.2015.55931/2015.55931.Census-Of-India-1901-Vol6-a-lower-Probinces-Of-Bengal-And-Their-Leudatoties-Pt2-imperial-Tables_djvu.txt).

The four male literate age counts add to 54,415 in Purnea, 42,419 in Sonthal Parganas, and 81,078 in Balasore. Their separate male illiterate counts also give these results. The Balasore age rows give 2,408 literate women and the Sonthal Parganas age rows give 2,097 literate women.
