# Canada 1901 occupations

The Census and Statistics Office published the 1901 returns in 1910 in *Occupations of the People*, Bulletin XI. Table I is on printed pages 1–3, PDF pages 15, 17 and 19. The French edition has the same table on printed pages 25–27. The 1901–03–31 all-resident denominators come from *Fourth Census of Canada*, volume I, Table I, printed pp. 2–5.

`occupation_1901_table_i.csv` gives the printed total column. The original table counts a person's recorded occupation. It includes paid workers, own-account workers and people in other occupations without reported wages or time employed. It does not count all residents or allocate dependants to occupations. Its original age columns divide at 16 and it has separate columns by sex. This extraction keeps both sexes and both age groups combined.

The ten printed classes sum to 1,866,129 people across the nine mutually exclusive regions. The non-productive class comprises 69,201 chiefly retired persons, and the other nine classes sum to 1,796,928. The value printed for the productive subtotal in the introduction is 1,790,928. Use the Table I cells, which independently close by region and by class to the Table I national totals.

The opening assigns Table I to the eight existing opening accounts. Seven rows match census territory directly. The 1901 Organized Territories account is the four electoral territories summarized as Northwest Territories in volume I, 158,940 people. Quebec province (1,648,898) and Yukon (27,219) contribute their observed classes to the Quebec and Unorganized Territories account. The rest of the Unorganized Territories (25,490) have no Bulletin XI row. The whole combined denominator is 1,701,607.

The observed unknown count is the all-resident denominator minus the nine occupational classes. It includes people with no recorded occupational class, the non-productive class retained in a separate field, and 25,490 residents without a territorial observation. It has no unemployment interpretation. Opening counts apply each published class share and the unknown share to the January 1900 account.
