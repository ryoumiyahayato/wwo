# Hungarian Crown county population, 1890 and 1900

`hungarian_counties_1890_1900.csv` transcribes the 1890 and 1900 columns under *Polgári és katonai népesség* (civilian and military population together). The counts record population present at midnight ending 31 December. The rows are counties and county-level cities in *f) Tisza-Maros szöge* and *Horv.-Szlav.-orsz.*. The source lists the cities as separate jurisdictions. Modrus-Fiume county is in Croatia-Slavonia. The separately listed city and district of Fiume are in the Hungary panel.

Each group sums to the existing WWO parent at both census dates. The CSV gives the source endpoints and opening unit IDs. The opening figures above are the existing WWO control values.
