# Faiyum districts, 1897 and 1907

Itsa, Faiyum and Sinuris close to the independent provincial controls at both dates, including each sex. Two-date comparisons come from the 1907 census, Table 5, printed p. 32. The district centres are included in the markaz populations. [Official census page](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/api/image/books/princeton_aco003741/58/full/1600,/0/default.jpg)

The 1897 material classifies religion and whether residents had a reported occupation. These are separate distributions, and a reported occupation does not establish paid work. Itsa and Sinuris reading and writing rows close for both sexes. Faiyum markaz literacy remains unassigned because the male cells exceed the district male total by 36. Exact ages are unknown.
