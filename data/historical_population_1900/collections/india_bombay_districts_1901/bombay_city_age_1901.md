# Bombay City age and sex, 1901

*Census of India, 1901*, Vol. I-A, Special Tables for Cities, Table VII, printed p. 438, Bombay, columns 2–4. The table reports completed-age bands and unspecified ages. The age bands sum to 776,006 persons, 479,786 males and 296,220 females. [Original scan](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.55924/page/n440/mode/1up).

The census office explains the city tables' exclusion of cantonments on p. 435. The Bombay age table agrees exactly with the persons, males and females printed in Vol. IX-A, Bombay, Part II, Tables I and II for Bombay City. Table II, pp. 6–7, row 3 gives 821,764 persons in 1891, 518,093 males and 303,671 females, on the 1901 census geography.
