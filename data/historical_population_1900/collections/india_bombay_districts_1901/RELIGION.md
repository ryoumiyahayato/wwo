# Bombay City religion, 1901

The city census uses the 776,006 people actually counted, as specified on pp. 8–9. Its religion table on p. 24 and the government gazetteer's separate Jews and Others figures close to the 1901 endpoint of the opening account. The 1900 opening holds the city's fractions constant, allocates to 781,314 residents, and leaves the unspecified Others category unassigned. The census reports that plague affected the census population. Its impact on the fractions at opening is unquantified.
