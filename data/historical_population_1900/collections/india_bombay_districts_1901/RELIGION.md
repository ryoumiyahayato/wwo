# Bombay City religion, 1901

The city census uses the 776,006 people actually counted, as specified on pp. 8–9. Its religion table on p. 24 and the government gazetteer's separate Jews and Others figures close to the 1901 endpoint of the opening account. The 1900 opening holds the city's fractions constant, allocates to 781,314 residents, and leaves the unspecified Others category unassigned. The census reports that plague affected the census population. Its impact on the fractions at opening is unquantified. [City census, Vol. XI Part V](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/31004.pdf) · [Maharashtra government gazetteer, Census of 1901](https://gazetteers.maharashtra.gov.in/cultural.maharashtra.gov.in/english/gazetteer/Bombay%20City/volume_1/Census%20of%201864%2C1872%2C1881%2C1891%20And%201901.html)

The v40 model uses the same 776,006-person scope and sex controls in the all-India city's table. Its Sikh, Buddhist and Animistic counts account for 486 of the 639 people set aside in v39. The all-India table combines Jews with other religions in a 5,510-person column. The gazetteer's 5,357 Jews leave 153 people unassigned in the reference.
