# 1901 Scotland and Ireland census occupations

Census date 31 March 1901. The General Register Office's *Census of England and Wales, 1901. General Report with Appendices*, Appendix A, Table 35, prints the condensed occupation classification for the United Kingdom. The absolute both-sex counts appear on printed pages 270, 272, 274, 276, 278 and 280. The 1901 counts occur first at each row. The printed England and Wales column remains for reconciliation only.

I–XXII sum to the separately printed 1,982,812 for Scotland and 1,949,607 for all Ireland. XXIII records residents in the table without a specified occupation or unoccupied. The scope is nominally age 10 and above. The printed note on pp. 270–271 says the 1901 figures for Ireland include occupied children below age 10. `outside_occupation_table` is the difference from the full census denominator. The census total is 4,472,103 in Scotland and 4,458,775 in Ireland. The proposed occupation marginal uses only I–XXII for named counts and assigns XXIII and the scope difference to unknown.

Accounts are `uk_constituent_scotland` and `uk_constituent_ireland`. They open at 4,416,526 and 4,489,395 persons. Scale each account separately over its all-ages denominator. Use an independent `uk_1901_general_report_occupation_orders` classification. The General Report regrouped subcategories to approximate comparability across constituencies. Order IV excludes domestic outdoor service, Order VI includes some domestic outdoor occupations, and X–XXII also contain explicit regroupings. The archived England and Wales and Anglesey series use the separate Summary Tables Table XXXV and Welsh county report returns.
