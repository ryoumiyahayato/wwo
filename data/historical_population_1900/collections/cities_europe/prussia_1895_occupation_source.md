# Kingdom of Prussia occupational census, 14 June 1895

Table I, pp. 364–365, row `Staat`, gives principal occupations for 31,490,315 people present in Prussia on 14 June 1895. The trade journal *Zeitschrift des Oberschlesischen Berg- und Hüttenmännischen Vereins* (1896, nos. 10–11) reproduced the official preliminary returns. The Imperial Statistical Office's *Statistisches Jahrbuch für das Deutsche Reich 1900*, section I.11, p. 10, confirms the all-person denominator, the 12,855,755 workers including domestic servants and the residual totals for Prussia using the published census volumes 104 and 105. [Contemporary Table I](https://sbc.org.pl/Content/429814/PDF/iii4217-1896-10_11-0001.pdf), [Official statistical yearbook](https://digi.bib.uni-mannheim.de/fileadmin/statjahrb/514401303_1900/514401303_1900.pdf)

A–E are persons recorded in a principal occupation. G is the mutually exclusive category of domestic servants resident in the employer's household. The official yearbook counts G among workers. The occupational marginal leaves F, the 1,221,598 people in the combined no occupation or no occupational statement category, and H, the 17,412,962 dependents with no principal occupation, unknown. The CSV retains F and H separately.
