# Prussian mining, manufacturing and construction employment position, 14 June 1895

The 1896 contemporary reprint of Prussian census results, *Zeitschrift des Oberschlesischen Berg- und Hüttenmännischen Vereins*, nos. 10–11, printed p. 361, gives employment position within occupation B. The extract uses only column 2, `im Hauptberuf`. The seven entries sum to 3,994,407 male plus 761,448 female people, or 4,755,855 in all, the same total as occupation B in Table I. Side occupations, dependents and household servants attached to these workers are separate columns. [Contemporary reprint](https://sbc.org.pl/Content/429814/PDF/iii4217-1896-10_11-0001.pdf)

The seven codes can refine the existing B occupation without adding people. As an all-person marginal scoped to industrial employment position, the denominator is Prussia's 31,490,315 persons present and the unknown remainder is 26,734,460. Category `a` combines independents and managers. Categories `b1`–`b3` separate technical, supervisory and administrative roles. Codes `c1`–`c3` separate working household members, trained workers including apprentices, and miscellaneous labour and service roles. The source does not enumerate industrial property ownership in this table.
