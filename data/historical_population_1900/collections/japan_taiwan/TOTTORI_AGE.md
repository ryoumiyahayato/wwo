# Tottori resident ages around 1900

The Tottori statistical yearbooks report the local resident population by age and sex. The 1902 return has 214,363 males and 215,381 females, including 96 males and 57 females without a stated age. The 1903 main return has 216,317 males and 217,439 females, including 106 males and 60 females without a stated age. Six male and one female foreign residents are enumerated separately in 1903. The two dated controls describe different populations. [1902 Tables 34–35](https://www.pref.tottori.lg.jp/secure/618481/b1902m35_04_jin.pdf#page=17) · [1903 Tables 37–38](https://www.pref.tottori.lg.jp/secure/618482/b1903m36_04_jin_1.pdf#page=19)

The opening balance and sex margins retain the corrected cabinet series. Its 1899 Tottori control is 206,200 males and 204,900 females. Within each sex, use the nearer 1902 local age shares, retaining the unreported ages. A counted age of one denotes the birth year. At year end the reported age minus one approximates completed age. The 1903 table is an independent comparison. Local counts and the corrected cabinet series use different residence definitions. Births, deaths, migration and register scope changes over the three-year transfer are unquantified. [Cabinet resident population sex tables](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/805921/1/13)

The source records single years. The opening retains ages zero to four separately and groups older ages in five-year intervals. Across the three broad bands (0–14, 15–59, 60 and over) the largest change in one sex between the two local returns is 0.270 percentage points, for girls aged 0–14. This measures the adjacent-year difference. Dates recorded before the 1873 calendar change add uncertainty at the oldest ages.
