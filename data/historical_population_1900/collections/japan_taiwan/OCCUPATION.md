# Japanese home-island employment in 1900

Saito and Settsu report LTES principal-occupation shares in Table 3. For both sexes combined the 1900 secondary and tertiary shares are 17.0% and 19.8%. The primary-sector share is their complement, 63.2%. Appendix B, Table B.1 estimates 24,516,000 gainfully occupied people in total. Its method shifts weight between sectors for people with subsidiary occupations while preserving the aggregate. Accordingly, WWO applies the principal-occupation shares to that aggregate.

The worker-equivalent column records the separate series in Appendix B. It is context for sectoral productive capacity in calendar 1900. The weights approximate the contribution of subsidiary work and do not measure hours, output, or unique people per sector.

The census scopes contain all 47 home-island prefectures. The occupation estimates are admitted only at the combined scope. Prefectural employment is unassigned. Taiwan is outside the scope. Unclassified residents include people outside gainful occupation; their status is unspecified in this distribution.
