# Japanese prefecture resident sex controls

The Cabinet Statistics Bureau's corrected residence series (乙種), published in 1909, includes sex columns for the 47 historical prefectures. At December 31, 1899, the published sums across prefectures match the separately printed totals, 22,338,100 male, 21,931,800 female and 44,269,900 overall. Every prefecture's sex sum matches its own printed control for that date. Figures are rounded to hundreds. The register excludes foreigners outside the Japanese household registration and covers Japan proper. The 1898, 1900 and 1903 source columns stay in the raw files with their original rounding discrepancies. [Government facsimile, printed pp. 14–19](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/805921/1/13) · [National control p. 19](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/api/iiif/805921/R0000015/full/3800,/0/default.jpg)

The opening retains 44,012,686 residents. For each prefecture, apportion its male and female counts separately across the same-sex ages of the 1899 LTES national reconstruction. Carry those modeled shares into the existing prefecture balance. Combining single years from age five into five-year bands follows the existing opening method. The LTES totals are 21,779,426 male and 21,741,469 female, 749,005 fewer residents overall than the 乙種 series. The model uses LTES ratios within sex. Tokyo's source sex counts are 1,035,900 male and 911,400 female. Osaka's are 784,700 male and 737,000 female. Variation in age within each prefecture still requires local age returns. Subnational residence estimates, foreign population and age shares have no measured opening error interval. [LTES Manpower Tables 3-1 and 3-2](https://d-repo.ier.hit-u.ac.jp/records/2035738)
