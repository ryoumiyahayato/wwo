WWO Cabinet shared miniature modules, R12

Original procedural meshes generated by tools/map/build_settlement_modules.py
with local Blender 5.2.1 LTS. No third-party models, bitmap textures or copied
building facades. Six named meshes share one vertex-colour masonry material.
436 triangles, 39,104 bytes in cabinet_building_modules.glb.

Models: paris_mansard (104), london_terrace (128), rural_house (32),
civic_hall (68), warehouse (44), quay_platform (60).
Metres; bottom origin; glTF Y-up. Generic late-19th-century massing only.
No claim of measured dimensions or precise identity for any named building.
No animation, skeleton, interior or collision. Rural module remains unplaced.

Local placement descriptions are in the existing Paris / London content shards.
All module offsets are explicitly ABSTRACT_MINIATURE_COMPOSITION, not 1900
cadastre, real building ownership, census dwelling counts or transport capacity.
See docs/map/local_settlement_miniatures_r12.md for locality sources and gaps.
