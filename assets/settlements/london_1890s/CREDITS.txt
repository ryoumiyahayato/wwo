London historical miniature presentation

BUILDING FOOTPRINTS
A Layer of Late Victorian London: The Building Footprints from the 1:1,056
Ordnance Survey Map (1891-1896), corrected version corr_v1.
Remi Petitpierre, Isabella di Lenardo, Polly Hudson, Hendrik Herold,
Katherine McDonough, Beatrice Vaienti, Robert Hecht and Christopher Fleet.
https://zenodo.org/records/21282700
https://doi.org/10.5334/johd.602
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: read EPSG:3857, convert to a local geographic map chart; discard very
small fragments; raster generalisation and bounded closing; preserve source
voids, polygon holes and selected spatial corridors; triangulate and partition
into 3 km tiles. Runtime meshes are display derivatives, not source buildings.
Actual corrected layer read: 1,299,040 features. The source description also
contains an older total of 1,299,029. Survey 1891-1895, publication 1893-1896.
The source reports six missing sheets and extraction/sheet-edge limitations.
No building use, historical height, ownership or road capacity is inferred.
The 8 m block height and map colours are presentation assumptions.

HISTORICAL MAP REFERENCE / SELECTED PRIMARY STRUCTURE
Ordnance Survey London town plans, National Library of Scotland.
https://maps.nls.uk/os/townplans-england/london-1056-1890s.html
https://maps.nls.uk/view/101202420
CC-BY (NLS). Selected corridors, bridges and open spaces are cartographically
generalised reference inputs. Their display widths are not surveyed property
boundaries. Other gaps in the building layer are not automatically roads.

MODERN WATER
Copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Selected Thames water polygons, park lakes and surviving dock basins from the
OSM API, acquired 2026-10-30. Individual object IDs, versions and timestamps
are retained in index.json. Modern water is not an 1890s bank survey.
Changes: union only supplied neighbouring polygons; preserve islands and
holes; simplify at 1.5 m; triangulate; add map-colour inset and a 3 m bank band.
Colour variation is not measured water depth. Historic infilled dock basins
are not reconstructed. Small tributaries and all minor lakes are incomplete.

The raw corrected GeoPackage and GIS preparation inputs are not shipped.
The miniature creates no economic, demographic, land or transport authority.
