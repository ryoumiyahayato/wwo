WWO R56 terrain and coast display derivatives, 2026-10-19.

This package retains R42-R55 notices and original sources. Terrain, land cover
and coast are physical display references. They do not establish 1900 land
use, reservoirs, sovereignty, ownership, military control or economic output.

Height: NOAA NCEI ETOPO2022 (CC0/public domain); Copernicus DEM GLO-90.
Copernicus DEM copyright DLR e.V. (2010-2014), Airbus Defence and Space GmbH
(2014-2018). COPERNICUS: European Union / ESA.
The public GLO90 COG's actual affine and vertical datum are retained:
WGS84 geographic horizontal coordinates, EGM2008 orthometric metres; DSM.
The regional extracts are averaged to6arcseconds and displayed at1/512degree.
This is not90m or metre-scale LOCAL terrain throughout the display domain.

Finite demonstration areas use2250 categorical samples per3degree tile.
Palette colours are area-reduced. Class IDs are never linearly interpolated.
Elsewhere the available225/450/2250 samples retain their source limitations.

Physical coast: Natural Earth5.1.2 public domain is retained globally.
Crete REGION medium additionally uses finite whole GSHHG2.3.7 full-resolution
island components, compiled2017-06-15, based on heterogeneous legacy WVS.

GSHHG is LGPL3-or-later. Its LICENSE.TXT, README.TXT, COPYING.LESSERv3.txt
and COPYINGv3.txt accompany this distribution. Modified Crete physical
derivatives retain that license; the separate historical CShapes contribution
retains CC BY-NC-SA4.0 and its existing notices.
The finite unmodified source components and modification/compilation scripts
are supplied beside the internal preview in sources/r56. They are not needed
at runtime and are not embedded in the game. Original global research inputs
remain in the development workspace. No positional metre accuracy or exact
1900 shoreline is asserted. Original formal land and political records stay
unchanged. Newly displayed physical land outside the historical source mask
is not automatically assigned to a state.
