# Historical representative assets — attribution

All downloaded SVGs are unmodified. Godot rasterizes each complete flag. Reconstruction uncertainties and presentation approximations are retained in political_visuals_1900.json.

## Jabal Shammar representative — Arabia Display Reset R1

Asset: `jabal_shammar_representative.svg` (complete flag, downloaded unmodified).

Author: Guilherme Paula; simplified by NikNaks in 2019.

License: [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

[Original file and history](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_the_Emirate_of_Ha%27il.svg).

Clean red/yellow crescent and eight-pointed star design associated with Ha'il / Jabal Shammar. The repository uses it as an approximate representative visual with low confidence, not proof of the exact flag flown on 1900-01-01. The SVG contains only flag graphics, no lettering, scan background, caption or watermark.

The old green/purple livery asset and its generation path are retired. The underlying contemporary Zwemer evidence, local-authority notes and source illustration remain in historical content. Its historical colour account is not deleted or replaced by a new sovereignty assertion.

## bahrain_authority_1900 — M2.6

Author: WWO; original SVG consists of simple red and white rectangles.

[Historical red/white flag variant](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Bahrain_(1820%E2%80%931932).svg). The source description identifies a de facto 1910 variant despite its 1820 filename. Exact 1900 panel proportions remain unverified. A straight white hoist panel and red field are used as APPROXIMATE_HISTORICAL_VISUAL / AUTHORITY_LIVERY; no modern serrations and no claim of an exact 1900 national flag. No Commons SVG or photograph was copied.

## Arabia independent representative flags — M2.6.4

The following complete SVGs are WWO-authored geometric reconstructions. No
external artwork was downloaded or copied for this closure. The historical
design families are representative selection choices, not newly verified
historical evidence; dates are not assertions about the flags flown on
1900-01-01. All records set `approximate = true`, low/medium confidence and
`historical_national_flag = NOT_ASSERTED`. Exact proportions, lettering and
badge details remain approximate. New historical research is outside scope.

| Asset | Representative choice | Confidence / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| `hejaz_representative.svg` | Sharifian 1917 black/green/white flag with red hoist triangle | Medium; nearby-date flag, no backdated independent kingdom |
| `hasa_representative.svg` | Regional red/white Wahhabi standard, shahada | Low; already acquired 1900 plate supports regional association, not Ottoman Hasa's exact administrative flag |
| `yemen_representative.svg` | Later Yemeni imamate red standard, five stars and sword | Low; 1927 design family, simplified sword |
| `trucial_representative.svg` | Treaty-coast/Qasimi red flag within white border | Medium; local representative, not a unified national flag |
| `kuwait_representative.svg` | Local red flag with كويت inscription | Medium; 1914 design family, outlined Arial lettering |
| `qatar_representative.svg` | Local maroon flag with white serrated hoist | Low; 1930s design family, nine teeth not asserted for 1900 |
| `hadramaut_representative.svg` | Later Quaiti blue/red flag, red hoist triangle and white star | Low; 1939 design family, macro representative without changing Kathiri/Quaiti records |
| `mahra_representative.svg` | Later Mahra-associated green/white/blue flag, red hoist triangle | Low; later associated design, not a nearby-date exact flag |
| `aden_representative.svg` | Later colonial Blue Ensign with dhow badge | Low; 1937 design family, simplified complete ensign, not exact for the treaty hinterland |

The remaining Arabia visuals retain the existing Oman plain-red and Bahrain
red/white complete assets. The old Quaiti 1880 asset is preserved as evidence
but is no longer the macro display choice, since its mostly plain-red field
does not separate Hadramaut from the neighbouring red-field authorities.

Kuwait and Al-Hasa lettering is embedded as clean vector paths, originally
outlined from the installed Arial font. The superseded M2.6.4 generation tools
are removed; they are not an alternate display implementation. The illustration is public-domain historical material;
the simple vector artwork is project authored, with no third-party flag SVG
license dependency introduced.
